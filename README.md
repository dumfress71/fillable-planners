# Fillable Planners

Improved **fillable PDF** versions of selected practical workbooks by **Dustin Carl Umfress**.

## Available Fillable PDFs

| Planner | Category | Pages | Form Fields |
|---------|----------|-------|-------------|
| Daily Command Planner | Productivity | 7 | 59 |
| Habit Tracker System | Life & Wellness | 7 | 124 |
| One-Page Business Plan | Business | 7 | 52 |
| Personal Budget Workbook | Money | 7 | 88 |

> The actual PDF files are available in the local `pdfs/` folder of this project (or download them from the conversation artifacts). Upload them to the `pdfs/` directory in this repo to enable the download links on the landing page.

## How to use

1. Download any PDF.
2. Open in Adobe Acrobat Reader, Preview (macOS), Chrome, Edge, or any modern PDF viewer that supports AcroForm fields.
3. Click into the fields and type (or print and write by hand).
4. Save the PDF to keep your answers.

## Structure of each workbook

Every planner follows a consistent, practical flow:

1. **Cover** – Name, date, focus
2. **How to use** – Quick guidance
3. **Starting Point** – Honest reflection prompts
4. **Make a Plan / Define** – Concrete choices
5. **Action / Tracker** – Checklists or daily tracking
6. **Log** – Record what actually happened
7. **Review & Adjust** – Turn experience into next steps
